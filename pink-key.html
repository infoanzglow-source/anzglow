Pink Key Liquid Lipstick gives you rich, ultra-matte colour in a single stroke. The lightweight formula feels comfortable and non-drying, so colour stays put without that tight, cakey feel.

Choose from 12 shades — soft pinks like Nude Pink and Baby Pink, warm nudes like Caramel Pink and Brown Nude, and bold statements like Deep Rose and Classic Red. Every shade comes in our signature crystal-cut, rose-gold bottle.

  1. Start with clean, dry lips. A little lip balm first helps on very dry days — blot before applying.
  2. Use the soft-tip applicator to outline the centre of your upper lip, then follow your natural lip line.
  3. Fill in from the outside towards the centre. Press lips together lightly.
  4. Let it set for about 30 seconds for the full matte finish. Add a second layer for deeper colour.
